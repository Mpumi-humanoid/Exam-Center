How it works

From capture to record, in three steps

Every result moves through the same verified path before it reaches a student's screen.

01

Lecturer captures

Admins upload or enter marks per module, checked against a valid 0–100 range before saving.

02

System validates

Marks are stored against the student and module record, with an audit trail of any changes.

03

Student retrieves

Students log in and see only their own results, with an automatically calculated average.
